Privacy Policy

Desktop Assistant · Effective October 10, 2026

Desktop Assistant is a personal, non-commercial application built and used only by its developer, Min Thaw Phyo. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only): event titles, times, locations and free/busy information, using the calendar.readonly scope. The app cannot create, change or delete events.
  • No other Google data is requested.

How the data is used

  • Calendar data is used only to answer the developer's own questions about their schedule, such as upcoming events or free time.
  • Calendar data is processed on the developer's own computer by a locally running AI model.
  • Calendar data is not sent to third-party AI services, advertisers or any other external party.

Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens and any logs are stored only on the developer's own computer and are never published or shared.
  • No calendar data is stored on any server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
